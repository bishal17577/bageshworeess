About Our School

Established in 2045 BS, Bageshworee Secondary School has been serving the community of Benighat Rorang for over 38 years. Our school is recognized by the Government of Nepal and affiliated with the National Examination Board (NEB).

Our Vision

"To become a center of academic excellence that nurtures knowledgeable, skilled, and ethical citizens who contribute positively to society."

Our Mission

  • Provide quality education accessible to all students
  • Foster critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving skills
  • Integrate ICT in teaching and learning processes
  • Promote moral values, discipline, and social responsibility
