Welcome to Bageshworee School

📢 Important Notice: All student need to attend regular class.

Bageshworee Secondary School is one of the most popular and respected schools in the Dhading District. Located in a peaceful learning environment, our school is dedicated to providing quality education and shaping the future of young minds. We offer both Science Faculty and Management Faculty, allowing students to choose a path that suits their interests and career goals. With experienced teachers, well-maintained classrooms, laboratories, and a supportive academic atmosphere, we focus on academic excellence, discipline, and overall student development. We warmly welcome students and parents to join our educational family.

Our school offers:

  • Classes 1 to 12
  • Physic, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Labs
  • Library with 5000+ books
  • Sports facilities
  • Transportation service by bus

About Our School

Bageshworee Secondary School is a leading educational institution in Dhading District, offering Science and Management faculties. We are committed to quality education, discipline, and holistic student development in a supportive learning environment.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a center of excellence in education that nurtures knowledgeable, skilled, and ethical students. We aim to inspire lifelong learning, innovation, and leadership, preparing our students to succeed in a competitive world and serve society responsibly.
